HarborLink Infrastructure & Waterfront Development

This master plan outlines Solomon Kin LLC's (operating via the HarborLink Partners initiative) vision for zero-carbon, dual-use maritime real estate along the Delaware River. It details the integration of Underwater Data Centers (UDCs), hydrokinetic energy generation, and commercial marine logistics into active Philadelphia port and waterfront properties.

Strategic Development Prototyping

Institutional investors, federal grant reviewers, and agencies like PhilaPort require concrete, actionable deployment strategies. By naming specific, currently available real estate parcels, we demonstrate that Solomon Kin LLC has performed localized economic reconnaissance and possesses a defined master plan.

Standard warehouse or marina development is highly competitive. By introducing sub-pier data centers cooled by the river and powered by tidal micro-turbines, we elevate these projects from "standard real estate" to "critical national digital infrastructure." This uniquely positions HarborLink to capture lucrative tech-focused and clean-energy government grants that traditional developers cannot access.

Delaware River Site Prototypes

Operating under the HarborLink Partners initiative, Solomon Kin LLC is actively pursuing site control (Options/LOIs) and grant funding for zero-carbon, water-cooled digital infrastructure along the Delaware River.

Prototype 1: Piers 38 & 40 (South Philadelphia Port Area)

Maritime Supply & UDC Hub

Core Facility Uses: 360,000 sq. ft. Maritime Supply, Cold-Storage Cruise Provisioning (supporting the Norwegian Cruise Line terminal), and Bonded Warehousing.

Data & Energy Integration: Modular, pressurized Sub-Pier UDC Units anchored beneath the pier structures. Powered by Hydrokinetic Turbine arrays installed in the deep-water berth slots. Direct water-heat exchangers cool high-density AI compute pods, while waste heat is redirected to pre-heat thermal systems for neighboring cold-storage facilities.

Prototype 2: Pier 5 (Penn’s Landing / Center City)

Eco-Marina & Edge Compute Pavilion

Core Facility Uses: Eco-Marina Slips, Passenger Terminal/Water Taxi Hub, and Waterfront Dining/Retail.

Data & Energy Integration: A localized Sub-Surface Edge Data Center submerged beneath the floating marina pontoon system, serving low-latency media and municipal data. Kinetic Micro-Turbines integrated into the pier’s pylon cluster pilings capture natural river currents and ship wake turbulence, directly recharging electric water taxis and marina vessels.

Prototype 3: 709 N. Penn St (Northern Liberties)

Gateway Mixed-Use & Sovereign Cloud

Core Facility Uses: High-Density Mixed-Use, Tech Commercial Office, Waterfront Retail, and Luxury Residential.

Data & Energy Integration: A hybrid Waterfront Sub-Basement & Pier UDC Vault submerged along the riparian edge. Sub-surface Hydrokinetic Energy Mats laid along the river floor convert flow movement into building micro-grid power, serving as a private sovereign cloud node for commercial tech tenants.
